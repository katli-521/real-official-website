# Project Overview

## What is this?

KAT.LI — the personal portfolio website of Katerina Li, an audio engineer, composer, and technologist. The site features a dark digital-space aesthetic with sharp rectangular corners, particle animations, and ambient audio. It showcases her music, film rescores, game audio, and personal story across multiple sections.

## Key Features

- **Particle Hero**: Title "KAT.LI" made of particles that scatter on hover and reform on leave
- **Ambient Audio**: Debussy-style ambient music box triggered by mouse movement over hero, with mute toggle
- **Floating Phrases**: Personal facts fade in/out near cursor while hovering the hero
- **Persistent Navigation**: Fixed nav bar on all pages with KAT back-link and 7 section links
- **Bio Section**: File-tree sidebar linking to 3 subpages (cross-disciplinary, CV, readme)
- **Music Section**: 6 tracks with audio player placeholders, credits, album art, and story notes
- **Film Section**: 3 film rescores with YouTube embed placeholders
- **Game Section**: Silent Hill f rescore with YouTube embed
- **Live & Interaction**: Placeholder sections for future content

## Target Users

Admissions officers, music industry professionals, collaborators, and anyone interested in Katerina Li's work as a musician, composer, and audio engineer.
